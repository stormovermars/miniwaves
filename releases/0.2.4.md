Per-tenant wave steps and an optional fanfare; no schema change, no migration.

- `mw tenant protocol SLUG [set STEP... | reset]` (and `--workspace`): wave steps per tenant; tenant > workspace > embedded default; show prints the source.
- `mw fanfare [set|test|fetch|list|stop]`: a clip when `mw wave close` succeeds, a finale when a tenant becomes finished (once per transition); off by default; detached playback that never fails a board command.
- `mw fanfare fetch` downloads public-domain, CC0 and CC-BY pairs labelled by film (2001, Apocalypse Now, The Lone Ranger, Die Hard ...) with a sha256 verified before the file is moved into place and full attribution (licence URL for CC BY); pairs the local player cannot play are skipped and marked `unplayable here`; copyrighted film scores are bring-your-own slots, never downloaded.
- `mw fanfare stop` kills players mw started (Ctrl-C does not stop a finale); paplay and Windows cannot trim and play the whole track.
- `mw tenant protocol SLUG` without set/reset takes no write locks.
