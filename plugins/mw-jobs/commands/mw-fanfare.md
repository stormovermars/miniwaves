Run `mw fanfare $ARGUMENTS` and show its output (no arguments: the current settings; `mw help fanfare` lists the rest).
mw fanfare is sound only: no banner, dialog or notification. Ctrl-C does not stop a finale; `mw fanfare stop` does.
